Hanover Reimbursements is provided for submitting and administering Hanover Chapel expense reimbursement requests.

Submitting a request

Submit accurate information and only receipts you are authorized to share. Check that the email address, amount, date and uploaded document are correct. Do not include unrelated sensitive information in a receipt upload. The form accepts one image or PDF per submission, up to 10 MB.

Review and payment

The treasurer is responsible for reviewing requests and reimbursement decisions. A saved submission or confirmation email does not itself approve an expense or promise payment. Contact the treasurer about eligibility, status, corrections or payment.

Confirmation and duplicates

Keep the form open while a submission is processing. If confirmation is delayed or the result is uncertain, contact the treasurer before submitting the same receipt again. A receipt may have been saved even if an email or connection fails.

Availability and changes

The application may be unavailable during maintenance or service interruptions. These terms and the privacy notice should be updated when the service changes.
