Hanover Reimbursements

Submit receipt images or PDFs for Hanover Chapel expense reimbursement.

The form collects the information the treasurer needs to review a request: your name and email, the event and store, receipt date, amount, and receipt file. After the request is saved, the app attempts to email a confirmation and receipt copy to the treasurer and the email address you provide.

Open reimbursement form

How requests are handled

Receipt files and reimbursement records are stored in the church's Google Drive and Google Sheets. The treasurer reviews requests and manages their status. Submitting a receipt does not itself approve payment.

Privacy and use

Read the privacy notice for data collection, storage and service-provider details, and the terms of use before submitting. Questions can be sent to hanoverchapelabc@gmail.com.
